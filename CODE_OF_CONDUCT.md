# Code of conduct

Unframed is built for everyone, and so is its community. We want taking part here to be welcoming, safe and respectful for all, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different opinions and experiences, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept constructive criticism, and own and learn from mistakes.
- Think about what's best for the whole community, not only yourself.

## What's not acceptable

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information, like an address or email, without their permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and anywhere someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience something that breaks this code, report it privately: use **Report content** in the menu of the comment, issue or pull request. Reports reach the maintainer, not the person you report. For something that isn't tied to a comment, or if it involves the maintainer, you can also [report it to GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Every report is taken seriously, reviewed fairly and kept confidential.

## What happens next

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may:

1. **Correct:** a private note explaining what was wrong, and a public apology where it fits.
2. **Warn:** with consequences for repeated behavior, such as a period without interacting in the project.
3. **Temporarily ban** someone after a serious or repeated violation.
4. **Permanently ban** someone for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards a person or group.

Comments, commits, code and issues that break this code may be edited, hidden or removed.
