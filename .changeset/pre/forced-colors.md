Support for Windows high contrast (forced colors mode). When the system forces its own colors, components no longer lose what was drawn with backgrounds or shadows: checked switches and checkboxes, the checked radio, the selected segment, tab, page, calendar day and menu or listbox option are shown in the system's selection color; slider tracks, progress bars, spinners, skeletons, avatars and badge dots keep their shape; focused fields get an outline; disabled controls turn the system's grey; and link buttons look like links. The default look doesn't change.
