`<uf-drawer>` floats a little away from the screen's edges, with a border and rounded corners all around, like the other panels. The new `--uf-drawer-gap` sets the space (12px by default); `--uf-drawer-gap: 0` puts it against the edge as before. `--uf-drawer-radius` now rounds every corner.
