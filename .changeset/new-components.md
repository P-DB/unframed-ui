New components: Badge (`<uf-badge>`), Avatar (`<uf-avatar>`), Card (`<uf-card>`), Table (`<uf-table>`) with sorting and custom cells, and Pagination (`<uf-pagination>`). They come with React, Vue and Angular wrappers, and the CLI can copy them. Tokens add `--uf-color-info` and `--uf-color-info-subtle`, and the icon set adds `user`.
