Popovers, menus, dropdowns, the combobox and date picker lists, and dialogs get an accent edge and glow in dark mode, like elevated cards, instead of a black shadow that barely shows. Two new theme tokens: `--uf-shadow-overlay` and `--uf-shadow-modal` (the usual soft shadows in light mode).
