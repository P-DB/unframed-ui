New Date picker (`<uf-date-picker>`): type a date in the page language's format or pick one from a calendar popup. The value is `YYYY-MM-DD`; `min`, `max`, `isDateDisabled` and `required` validate natively in forms; `v-model` and Angular forms work. The calendar is also its own element, `<uf-calendar>`. New translation keys for its texts, and for the combobox's result count, which was English only.
